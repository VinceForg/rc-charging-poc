	\section{Introduction}
	
	This document serves as a basic overview of the characteristics of a resistor-capacitor (RC) network. When a capacitor is in a RC system, it enables the capacitor to charge and it's voltage will rise according to this equation:
	
	\begin{equation}
		V(t) = V_0 
		\left( 1 - e^{-t/RC}
		\right), 
	\end{equation}
	
	where the product RC is in units of seconds and is called the time constant
	
	\begin{equation}
		\tau = RC,
	\end{equation}
